Description

A burger is a popular food made with a cooked meat patty placed between two halves of a bun. It is commonly served with toppings such as lettuce, tomatoes, onions, cheese, and sauces.

Burgers can be customized with different ingredients and toppings depending on personal preference. They are often served with french fries or other side dishes and can be enjoyed as a quick meal.

Ingridients:

  • Burger Buns
  • Ground Beef
  • Cheddar Cheese
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Mayonnaise
  • Ketchup
  • Salt
  • Black Pepper
  • Cooking Oil

Steps

  1. Season the ground beef with salt and black pepper.
  2. Shape the ground beef into burger patties.
  3. Heat a pan or grill over medium-high heat.
  4. Cook the patties until they are fully cooked.
  5. Place a slice of cheddar cheese on each patty and allow it to melt.
  6. Toast the burger buns slightly.
  7. Spread mayonnaise and ketchup on the buns.
  8. Place the lettuce,tomato and onion on the bottom bun.
  9. Add the cooked burger patty with melted cheese.
  10. Place the top bun over the patty.
  11. Serve the burger while warm.
