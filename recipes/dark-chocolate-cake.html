Description

Dark chocolate cake is a rich and moist dessert made with cocoa powder and dark chocolate. It has a deep chocolate flavor with a slightly bitter taste that balances well with its sweetness.

This cake is great for birthdays, special occasions, or simply as a dessert after a meal. It can be served on its own or topped with chocolate frosting, powdered sugar, or fresh fruit.

Ingridients

  • All-Purpose Flour
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder
  • Dark Chocolate
  • Sugar
  • Eggs
  • Milk
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Vanilla Extract
  • Baking Powder
  • Baking Soda
  • Salt
  • Hot Water

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven.
  2. Prepare the cake pan.
  3. Mix the dry ingridients.
  4. Melt the dark chocolate.
  5. Mix the wet ingridients.
  6. Combine the wet and dry ingridients.
  7. Add the hot water and mix until smooth.
  8. Pour the batter into the prepared pan.
  9. Bake until the cake is cooked through.
  10. Allow the cake to cool.
  11. Decorate with frosting if desired.
  12. Slice and served.
