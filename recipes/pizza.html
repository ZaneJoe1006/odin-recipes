Description

Pizza is a popular dish made with a baked dough crust topped with tomato sauce, cheese, and different toppings. It can have many flavors depending on the ingredients used, such as pepperoni, mushrooms, vegetables, or different kinds of meat.

Pizza is commonly served as a meal or snack and is enjoyed by people around the world. It is best served warm and can be enjoyed with different sauces or toppings according to personal preference.

Ingridients

  • Pizza Dough
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Mozarella Cheese
  • Pepperoni
  • Olive Oil
  • Salt
  • Italian Seasoning

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to a high temperature.
  2. Prepare the pizza dough and place it on a baking surface.
  3. Spread tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  4. Add mozarella cheese and your desired toppings.
  5. Sprinkle italian seasoning over the pizza.
  6. Place the pizza in the oven and bake until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted.
  7. Remove the pizza from the oven and let it cool for a few minutes .
  8. Slice the pizza and serve while warm.
